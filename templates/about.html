About Me

I’m a senior at the University of Maryland, College Park, studying marketing & information systems at the Robert H. Smith School of Business. During my time in college, I’ve been a very involved individual both academically and professionally. At the University of Maryland, I’m involved in a variety of organizations, including College Park Scholars - Business, Society, and Entrepreneurship, Schanwald Sports Management Program, and Strategic Design Fellows. Involvement in these programs has reinforced my passion for meaningfully contributing to organizations in marketing through data-backed decision-making.

Professionally, I’ve been fortunate to hold intern positions in a variety of roles, most recently with Mercedes-Benz at their United States headquarters in Atlanta, GA. This role allowed me to focus deeply on my planning and advertising skills, as I spent a large portion of my summer working closely with the agency team to approve a variety of designs and conduct research for future campaigns. The contribution I take the most pride in from my time at Mercedes-Benz was the creative direction for five television commercials for the 2027 Masters campaign, which I was able to present to executives and forward to the agency for implementation come next year!
